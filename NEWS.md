## 1. Lead — Kyiv's bridges hit in Russia's biggest barrage of November

- Overnight 19–20 Nov, Russia launched its largest missile-and-drone
  strike of November 2026 against Ukraine: **404 drones and 111
  missiles, including 27 ballistic** (84 cruise + 27 ballistic).
- Three bridges over the Dnipro in Kyiv were damaged —
  Podilsko-Voskresenskyi, Darnytskyi and Pivdennyi (Southern) — and
  energy, heating and water supplies were hit. **At least 8 people
  killed, nearly 30 injured** (Zelensky; some outlets later reported 9).
- **Two Russian missiles and a jet-powered drone crashed in Poland**
  (Nowy Lipowiec, Rudnik nad Sanem, Mołodycz), a NATO member. No
  casualties and no critical damage there; Poland called it another
  provocation and asked NATO for more air defence.

## 2. Diplomacy — Three leaders, one declaration, no plan for Ukraine

- Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin held their **first
  three-way summit in Shenzhen on 19 Nov** (about 90 minutes, on the
  APEC sidelines) and issued a joint peace declaration.
- The four points: reform the UN; increase production/supply of refined
  petroleum products; state consent and immunity for state officials,
  and that the ICC and similar bodies must not abuse jurisdiction; and
  that a nuclear war “cannot be won and must never be fought”, with
  dialogue on reducing nuclear risks.
- The declaration sets **no timetable and never mentions Russia's war
  in Ukraine**; Kyiv and European leaders called it largely symbolic.
- Sources: Aju Press, full text of the declaration, 20 Nov 2026
  (https://www.ajupress.com/view/20261120080631792); Particle News

## 3. Justice — World court: Myanmar committed genocide

- The International Court of Justice ruled **9–6 on Thursday 19 Nov**
  that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in northern
  Rakhine State in 2017 — **the first time the ICJ has held a state
  responsible for genocide** in its 80-year history. About 10,000
  Rohingya were found to have been killed; the court ordered
  reparations, investigation/prosecution of those responsible, and
  facilitation of the refugees' safe, voluntary return, with a report
  to the court within two years.
- Myanmar's military-backed government dismissed the verdict on
  Friday 20 Nov as “unfair” and not unanimous.
- Sources: CNN, 19 Nov 2026
  (https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/19/asia/icj-myanmar-genocide-rohingya-ruling-intl-hnk);
  Wall Street Journal
  (https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/world-court-finds-myanmar-committed-genocide-against-rohingya-6fa7e663);
  Reuters, 20 Nov 2026

## 5. Weather — Cyclone Akvan hits Odisha; 300,000 moved to safety

- Cyclone Akvan crossed the north Andhra Pradesh / south Odisha coast
  **near Gopalpur on Friday morning, 20 Nov** (landfall process
  ~07:30–11:20 IST), with sustained winds **70–80 km/h, gusting to
  90 km/h**, after weakening from a severe cyclonic storm.
- **About 300,000 people (2,98,676) were evacuated** to 2,500+
  relief camps; 423 rescue teams and 9,341 cyclone shelters were
  deployed. No major damage or loss of life was reported.
